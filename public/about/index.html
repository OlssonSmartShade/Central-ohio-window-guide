Central Ohio Window Guide

About this guide

Updated October 2026

Central Ohio Window Guide is published by Olsson Smart Shades, a window treatment company that designs and installs motorized shades, blinds, plantation shutters, and window film across Columbus and Central Ohio.

Why we built it

Homeowners ask us the same questions every week: what smart shades really cost, whether shutters are worth it, what's safe for kids and pets. These guides answer those questions plainly, with the real installed price ranges we see on jobs in Central Ohio.

Our disclosure

Because we install window treatments ourselves, we recommend our own services in these guides. We aim to keep the information accurate and useful whether or not you hire us. Prices are typical installed ranges, not quotes; an in-home measurement is the only way to get an exact number.
