    Frequently asked questions

    How much do window treatments cost per window in Columbus, Ohio?

    Installed, faux wood blinds typically run $125 to $500 per window, cordless cellular shades $175 to $750, manual roller shades $175 to $650, rechargeable motorized roller shades $450 to $1,300, and custom composite shutters $275 to $1,600.

    How accurate is this calculator?

    It gives a realistic budget range. The exact price depends on measured sizes, fabric or material, and options, and is confirmed with a free in-home measurement and written quote.
