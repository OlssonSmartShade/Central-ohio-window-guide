How to Choose a Window Treatment Company in Columbus

Custom window treatments are built to a measurement and installed in your home, so the company matters as much as the product. Ask these eight questions before you sign.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated November 20264 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    Choose a company that measures in your home, puts the quote in writing, installs with its own team, sells brands it is authorized for, and explains what the manufacturer warranty covers. Ask the eight questions below before you sign.

    8 questions to ask before you hire

    1. Who measures? Custom treatments are built to the measurement, so a professional in-home measure protects you if something doesn't fit.
    2. Will I get a written, itemized quote, and how soon? You should see each window, product and option on paper before you pay anything.
    3. Who installs: employees or subcontractors? Ask whether the same company that measured will install and stand behind the work.
    4. Are you an authorized dealer for the brands you sell? Authorized dealers sell genuine products with full manufacturer warranty support.
    5. What does the warranty cover? For example, Norman covers its products for life against defects for the original purchaser, motors for 5 years and cords for 7 years. Labor and trip charges are usually not included, so ask how the company handles them.
    6. Can I see real samples in my own light? Fabric looks different at noon than at dusk, and in a west room than a north one.
    7. What is the deposit and the lead time? Know how much is due up front and roughly when installation will happen.
    8. Can you handle every window? Arches, tall windows, sliding doors, motorization and window film are easier with one company than three.

    Red flags

    • A price given over the phone without anyone measuring
    • No written quote, or a quote without product names
    • Pressure to sign the same day for a "today only" price
    • Unclear answers about who installs

    Frequently asked questions

    Should I get more than one quote?

    Many homeowners compare two or three written quotes. Make sure each one lists the same products and options so the comparison is fair.

    How long does custom window treatment installation take?

    Custom products are made after measuring, which usually takes a few weeks; installation for most homes takes a day.
