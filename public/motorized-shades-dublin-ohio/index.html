Motorized Shades and Custom Window Treatments in Dublin, Ohio

A local guide to motorized shades, cellular shades, and plantation shutters for Dublin, Ohio homes, from Muirfield Village and Tartan West to Bridge Park condos: what works, what it costs, and HOA tips.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated October 20264 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    For most Dublin homes, motorized shades make the most sense on tall great-room and stairwell windows, cordless cellular shades on bedrooms and offices, and plantation shutters on street-facing front rooms. Expect roughly $450 to $1,300 per window for motorized shades installed, or $6,500 to $12,000 to motorize a typical 12-window home.

    What makes Dublin homes different

    Dublin has an unusually wide mix of housing, and each type has its own window challenges:

    • Muirfield Village and Tartan West: larger custom and semi-custom homes, often with two-story great rooms, arched transoms, and walls of glass facing wooded lots or golf course views. The goal is usually glare and heat control without blocking the view.
    • Ballantrae, Jerome Village edge, and newer western subdivisions: newer builds with open floor plans and big rear windows. Many homeowners move in with bare windows and want a whole-house plan done at once.
    • Bridge Park and the Bridge Street District: condos and townhomes with floor-to-ceiling glass, close neighbors, and strong afternoon sun. Privacy during the day and blackout for bedrooms are the main asks.
    • Historic Dublin and older neighborhoods: smaller, older windows, sometimes out of square. Inside-mount shutters and custom-measured shades fit these best.

    Smart, motorized, and automated shades in Dublin

    Motorized shades, also called automated blinds or automated window treatments, raise and lower by remote, phone, voice, or schedule. The Matter-over-Thread standard lets them work with Apple Home, Alexa, Google Home, and SmartThings at the same time, which matters in Dublin's many tech-heavy households. Rechargeable battery motors need no wiring and can be added to a finished home in an afternoon. Hardwired motors are the clean choice during a basement finish, remodel, or new build. More detail in our smart home window automation guide.

    Best picks by room

    • Two-story great rooms: motorized roller or solar shades, grouped so one button moves every window together.
    • West-facing rear windows and sunrooms: solar shades with a 3% to 5% openness to cut glare and heat while keeping the view. See sunroom window treatments.
    • Bedrooms and nurseries: blackout cellular shades, cordless or motorized. See child and pet safe options.
    • Front living and dining rooms: plantation shutters for curb appeal and a consistent look from the street.
    • Home offices: light-filtering shades on a schedule to stop screen glare in the afternoon.

    Not sure which style fits? Compare them in shades vs blinds vs shutters.

    HOA tips for Dublin homeowners

    Many Dublin neighborhoods have homeowner associations, and some have guidelines about what is visible from the street. Before ordering, check your HOA documents for rules on backing color or exterior appearance. White or neutral backings and white shutters almost always meet these guidelines, and a good installer will ask about this during the consultation.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best way to cover tall great-room windows?

    Motorized shades. The top rows of a two-story window can't be reached by hand, and a motor lets you control the whole wall with one button or a schedule.

    Do Dublin HOAs have rules about window treatments?

    Some do, usually about what shows from the street. Check your documents first; neutral backings and white shutters are almost always fine.

    Can I add motorized shades without rewiring?

    Yes. Rechargeable motors need no wiring and charge with a USB cable every few months.

    How long does it take?

    Custom shades usually take a few weeks to make after measuring, and installation for most homes is done in a day.
