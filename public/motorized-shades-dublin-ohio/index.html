Motorized Shades and Custom Window Treatments in Dublin, Ohio

Updated October 2026

Dublin homes range from established neighborhoods to newer builds with big window walls and two-story great rooms. This guide covers the window treatments that work best for Dublin homeowners, what they cost installed, and how to get them done right.

Smart and motorized shades in Dublin

Motorized shades raise and lower by remote, phone, voice, or schedule, and the Matter-over-Thread standard lets them work with Apple Home, Alexa, Google Home, and SmartThings at once. Rechargeable battery motors need no wiring, so they can be added to a finished home in an afternoon. Hardwired motors are the clean choice during a remodel or new build. Popular choices for Dublin homes

Motorized shades make tall great-room windows easy to control. Cellular shades insulate best through Ohio winters, solar shades cut glare in sunrooms, and plantation shutters add lasting curb appeal. Compare them in shades vs blinds vs shutters.

What it costs in Dublin

Motorized rechargeable roller shades typically run $450 to $1,300 per window installed, hardwired $650 to $1,600, cordless cellular shades $175 to $750, and custom composite shutters $275 to $1,600. Full breakdown: window treatment costs in Central Ohio.
