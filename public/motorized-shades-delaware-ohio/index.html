Window Treatments for New and Historic Homes in Delaware, Ohio

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

Delaware mixes fast-growing new subdivisions with historic homes near downtown and Ohio Wesleyan. New builds should plan for motorization before drywall and use temporary shades on move-in, while older homes need careful custom measuring. Expect roughly $175 to $750 per window for cordless cellular shades and $450 to $1,300 for rechargeable motorized shades, installed.

What makes Delaware homes different

  • New subdivisions: Delaware and the surrounding townships are among the faster-growing parts of Central Ohio, so many owners are covering an entire house at once.
  • Historic homes near downtown: older houses near Sandusky Street and the Ohio Wesleyan area often have tall, narrow, sometimes out-of-square windows.
  • Two-story great rooms: common in newer homes, where motorized shades solve windows nobody can reach.

New construction checklist

  1. Decide which windows you might ever motorize and prewire them before drywall.
  2. Measure after windows and trim are installed.
  3. Use temporary privacy shades on move-in day.
  4. Finish room by room if needed, starting with bedrooms and the main living area.

Tall and hard-to-reach windows

Motorized roller or cellular shades are the usual answer for two-story windows. They can run on a schedule, from a wall switch, or by voice with Alexa, Google Home, or Apple Home. See smart home window automation and hard-to-cover windows.

Frequently asked questions

How much do window treatments cost in Delaware, Ohio?

Faux wood blinds typically run $125 to $500 per window installed, cordless cellular shades $175 to $750, motorized rechargeable roller shades $450 to $1,300, and custom composite shutters $275 to $1,600.

Can I add motorized shades without rewiring?

Yes. Rechargeable battery motors give full app, voice, and schedule control with no new wiring. A charge typically lasts months between top-ups.

When should I measure windows in a new Delaware home?

After the windows and trim are installed, so measurements match the finished openings.

What's the best option for two-story windows?

Motorized shades, which run from a remote, wall switch, app, voice assistant, or schedule, so no ladder is needed.
