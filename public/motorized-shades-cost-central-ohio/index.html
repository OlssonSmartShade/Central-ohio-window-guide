How Much Do Motorized and Custom Shades Cost in Central Ohio?

Window treatment prices depend on three things: the size of the window, the fabric or material, and whether the shades are motorized. Below are real installed price ranges for Central Ohio homes in 2026, meaning measurement and installation are included, not just the product.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated October 20264 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    In Columbus and Central Ohio, expect roughly $125 to $650 per window for quality manual blinds and shades, $450 to $1,600 per window for motorized shades, and $275 to $1,950 per window for custom plantation shutters, all installed.

    Larger homes in areas like Dublin, New Albany, and Powell often have oversized and two-story windows, which push projects toward the top of these ranges.

    Motorized shades

    Motorized rechargeable roller shades typically run $450 to $1,300 per window installed. Hardwired versions run $650 to $1,600. Motorized cellular, or honeycomb, shades run about $475 to $1,400. The spread comes down to window size, fabric, and motor type.

    Rechargeable vs. hardwired: rechargeable motors are the go-to for existing homes because no electrician is needed. Hardwired motors make the most sense in new construction or a remodel, when wiring can go in before drywall. Smart home control through Alexa, Google Home, Apple Home, or a hub usually adds a small one-time cost for a bridge. Learn more in our guide to smart home window automation in Central Ohio.

    Manual roller and solar shades

    If you don't need motorization, manual roller shades run $175 to $650 per window installed. Solar shades, which cut glare and heat while keeping your view, fall in a similar range depending on the screen fabric. They're a popular pick for west-facing rooms and sunrooms.

    Cellular, or honeycomb, shades

    Cordless cellular shades run $175 to $750 per window. These are the insulating pick for Ohio's cold winters, since the honeycomb pockets trap air at the glass. Blackout fabric adds roughly 10 to 30 percent.

    Custom blinds

    Faux wood blinds run $125 to $500 per window installed, and real wood blinds $150 to $600. Cordless lifts, which keep cords out of reach of kids and pets, are available across the range. See our guide to child- and pet-safe window treatments.

    Plantation shutters

    Custom composite shutters run $275 to $1,600 per window, and real hardwood $325 to $1,950. Arched and specialty shapes run 15 to 40 percent more, since each one is built to the exact opening. Not sure which type fits your home? Compare them in shades vs. blinds vs. shutters.

    What changes the price

    Blackout fabric, specialty shapes, oversized windows, cassettes, and side channels all add cost. So do hard-to-reach windows that need extra installation time.

    How to save without cutting quality

    Motorize only the windows that benefit most, like tall, hard-to-reach, or daily-use windows, and go cordless manual everywhere else. Keep fabric choices consistent across rooms, and plan treatments during new construction, when hardwiring is cheapest.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does it cost to do a whole house in motorized shades in Columbus?

    A typical 12-window home in rechargeable motorized roller shades usually lands around $6,500 to $12,000 installed.

    Is it cheaper to motorize only some windows?

    Yes. Mixing motorized and cordless manual shades is one of the most common ways Central Ohio homeowners stay on budget.

    How do I get an exact price?

    Every window is a little different, so an in-home measurement is the only way to get a firm number.
