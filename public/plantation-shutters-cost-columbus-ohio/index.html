How Much Do Plantation Shutters Cost in Columbus, Ohio?

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

Custom plantation shutters in Columbus typically run about $400 to $675 per standard window installed for composite, and about $475 to $825 for hardwood. Small windows start around $275, and sliding patio doors run about $900 to $1,950. Your final price depends on size, shape, material, and options, so get a written quote after an in-home measurement.

What changes the price

  • Material: composite (vinyl-based) costs less and handles moisture, so it is the usual pick for kitchens and baths. Hardwood is lighter, takes stain, and suits wide windows and living spaces. Hybrid builds fall in between.
  • Size and panel count: wider windows need more panels and hinges, and very tall windows may need a divider rail.
  • Louver size: larger louvers give a more open view and a more modern look.
  • Frame and mounting: inside-mount frames need enough window depth; shallow windows need an outside-mount frame, which adds cost.
  • Shape: arches, half-rounds, and angled tops are built to a template of your exact opening.
  • Tilt style: a hidden tilt rod has a cleaner look than a front tilt bar.

Budgeting a whole house

Shutters on every window adds up quickly. Many Columbus homeowners put shutters in the rooms people see most, such as the front of the house, living room, and primary bedroom, then use cordless cellular shades or faux wood blinds in secondary rooms. As a rough example, 10 standard composite shutter windows typically land around $4,000 to $6,750 installed.

How long shutters take

Custom shutters are built to order after an in-home measurement. Lead times vary by line and season, commonly a few weeks to six weeks. Some standard lines can be expedited; premium hardwood lines usually take longer.

Are shutters worth it?

Shutters are a long-term choice. They have no cords, wipe clean, add an insulating layer in Ohio winters, and read as a built-in feature to buyers. If you plan to stay in the home for years, they are often the treatment homeowners are happiest with. If you want light control on very tall windows or prefer a softer look, motorized shades may fit better.

Frequently asked questions

How much do plantation shutters cost in Columbus, Ohio?

Custom composite shutters typically run about $400 to $675 per standard window installed, hardwood about $475 to $825, and sliding patio doors about $900 to $1,950. Small windows start around $275.

Are composite or wood shutters better?

Composite shutters handle humidity, so they suit kitchens and bathrooms. Hardwood shutters are lighter, can be stained, and work well on wide windows in living areas. Many homes use both.

Can shutters go on arched or odd-shaped windows?

Yes. Custom shutters can be built for arches, half-rounds, and angled tops after the installer measures or templates the exact opening.

Can you put shutters on a sliding patio door?

Yes. Bypass shutters slide on a track and bi-fold shutters fold to the side, so the door stays usable every day.
