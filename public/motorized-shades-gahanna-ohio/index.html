Energy-Saving and Motorized Window Treatments in Gahanna, Ohio

Updated October 2026

Many Gahanna homes were built before today's high-efficiency windows, so drafty glass and high heating bills are a common complaint. The right window treatment is one of the simplest fixes.

The insulation pick: cellular shades

Cellular, or honeycomb, shades trap air in their pockets and slow heat loss at the glass through Ohio winters. Double-cell and blackout versions insulate even more. Plantation shutters are a strong second, since they seal tightly inside the frame.

Let automation do the work

Motorized shades on a schedule close on cold nights and through hot summer afternoons without anyone remembering to do it, which is where the real savings come from. More in our smart home window automation guide.

What it costs in Gahanna
