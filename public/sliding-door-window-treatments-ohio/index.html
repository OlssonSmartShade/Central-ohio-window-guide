Window Treatments for Sliding Glass and Patio Doors

A door you walk through every day needs a treatment that gets out of the way. Here are the options that work best on sliding and patio doors in Central Ohio, and what they cost.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated November 20264 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    For a sliding door you use every day, pick something that moves sideways or stacks out of the way: vertical cellular shades, soft fabric vanes like Norman SmartDrape, sliding plantation shutter panels, or drapery. Roller shades also work if they are wide enough and you are happy raising them to walk through. Patio-door-size shutters run about $900 to $1,950 installed in Central Ohio.

    What makes sliding doors different

    • They are wide and tall, so one shade is large and heavy
    • People walk through them many times a day, so the treatment can't be in the way
    • The handle sticks out, so an inside-mounted shade can rub or snag
    • West- and south-facing patio doors bring in the most afternoon heat and glare

    Your options compared

    Should you motorize a patio door?

    Motorizing makes the most sense on large or heavy treatments, on doors you want to close every afternoon on a schedule, and when the patio door is part of a wall of glass. A motorized shade can lower on its own when the sun reaches the glass and raise again in the evening. See our sunroom and hard-to-cover windows guide for walls of glass.

    What it costs in Central Ohio

    • Plantation shutters for large windows and patio doors: about $900 to $1,950 composite and $1,100 to $1,950 hardwood, installed
    • Large manual roller shades: up to about $650 per shade installed; rechargeable motorized up to about $1,300
    • Large cordless cellular shades: up to about $750 installed
    • Exact pricing depends on width, height and fabric and is confirmed at an in-home measure

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best window treatment for a sliding glass door?

    One that moves sideways, such as vertical cellular shades, fabric vanes, or sliding shutters, so you can walk through without raising anything.

    Are vertical blinds outdated?

    Traditional plastic vertical blinds have largely given way to softer fabric options like vertical cellular shades and Norman SmartDrape, which slide the same way but look more like drapery.
