Motorized Shades and Custom Window Treatments in New Albany, Ohio

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

New Albany homes are well suited to whole-home motorized shades: tall windows, transoms, and large rooms are hard to manage by hand, and one tap or a sunrise schedule can move every shade at once. White shutters or neutral-backed shades on the front keep the classic Georgian facade uniform. Motorized shades run about $450 to $1,600 per window installed.

What makes New Albany homes different

  • Georgian and Federal-style architecture: New Albany is known for brick homes with symmetrical facades, white trim, and divided-light windows. Whatever goes inside the windows shows from the street, so a consistent exterior look matters.
  • Palladian, arched, and transom windows: common over entries, in two-story foyers, and in great rooms. These need custom shapes or motorization.
  • Large homes with many windows: 30 to 50 windows is not unusual, which makes scenes and schedules far more practical than opening shades one by one.
  • Ongoing new construction: newer neighborhoods continue to be built, giving owners the chance to plan hardwired motors and hidden shade pockets before drywall.

Whole-home smart shades

For larger homes, motorized shades on scenes and schedules are the standard: every shade in a room or the whole house moving together, by voice, app, wall keypad, or sunrise and sunset. Matter-over-Thread lets shades work with Apple Home, Alexa, Google Home, and SmartThings, and many systems also connect to professional whole-home control platforms. Hardwired motors fit new construction, while rechargeable motors retrofit finished rooms without wiring. More in our smart home window automation guide.

Keeping the facade uniform

In a symmetrical brick home, mismatched shades in the front windows stand out. Two simple rules keep it clean:

  • Use a white or neutral backing on every street-facing shade, even if the room-side fabric is a bold color.
  • Align bottom rails at the same height on scheduled shades, which motorization handles automatically.

White plantation shutters on front rooms are the other classic answer, and they match the white trim common on New Albany homes. Check any neighborhood association guidelines before ordering.

Best picks by room

  • Two-story foyers and great rooms: motorized roller or cellular shades, with arched tops left open or fitted with custom shapes.
  • Formal living and dining rooms: custom hardwood shutters or motorized Roman shades.
  • Primary bedrooms: motorized blackout cellular shades on a wake-up schedule.
  • Sunrooms and screened porches: solar shades for glare and heat. See sunroom and hard-to-cover windows.
  • Home theaters and media rooms: motorized blackout shades tied to a movie scene.

Frequently asked questions

How do you cover Palladian and arched windows?

Arched tops are usually left open or fitted with custom-shaped shutters, and the rectangular sections below get shades or shutters.

How do I keep the front of the house looking uniform?

White or neutral backings on every street-facing shade, or white shutters on front rooms.

Do motorized shades work with whole-home control systems?

Most do, through Matter and professional integrations. Confirm your system during the consultation.

When should I plan shades in a new build?

Before drywall, so wiring for hardwired motors and recessed shade pockets can be added cleanly.
