Motorized Shades and Custom Window Treatments in New Albany, Ohio

Updated October 2026

New Albany's Georgian-style homes and newer construction feature tall windows, transoms, and large openings that suit motorization. This guide covers what works best, what it costs installed, and how to get it done right.

Whole-home smart shades

For larger homes, motorized shades on scenes and schedules are the standard: every shade in a room or the whole house moving at once, by voice, app, or sunrise and sunset. Hardwired motors fit new construction, and rechargeable motors retrofit finished rooms without wiring. More in our smart home window automation guide.

Popular choices for New Albany homes

Motorized roller and cellular shades for tall and transom windows, custom hardwood shutters for formal front rooms, and solar shades for sunrooms. See sunroom and hard-to-cover windows.

What it costs in New Albany

Motorized rechargeable roller shades typically run $450 to $1,300 per window installed, hardwired $650 to $1,600, motorized cellular $475 to $1,400, and hardwood shutters $325 to $1,950. Full breakdown: window treatment costs in Central Ohio.
