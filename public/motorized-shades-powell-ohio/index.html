Motorized Shades and Custom Window Treatments in Powell, Ohio

Updated October 2026

Powell homes often have open floor plans, sunrooms, and tall windows that are hard to reach without a ladder. Those are exactly the spaces where the right treatment, and usually a motor, makes the biggest difference.

High windows and great rooms

If a window needs a ladder, motorize it. Motorized roller or cellular shades run by remote, app, voice, or a sunrise and sunset schedule. Solar-powered motors suit sunny high windows where wiring isn't practical. More in our smart home window automation guide.

Sunrooms and open layouts

Solar shades cut glare and heat while keeping the view, and grouping every shade in an open living area on one scene keeps the look consistent. See sunroom and hard-to-cover windows.

What it costs in Powell

Motorized rechargeable roller shades typically run $450 to $1,300 per window installed, hardwired $650 to $1,600, and motorized cellular $475 to $1,400. Full breakdown: window treatment costs in Central Ohio.
