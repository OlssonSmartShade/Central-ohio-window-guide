Blackout Shades for Bedrooms in Central Ohio

Blackout fabric is only half the answer. Here is how to get a bedroom truly dark, which shade type to choose, and what it costs in Columbus and Central Ohio.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated November 20264 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    For the darkest bedroom, choose blackout cellular or blackout roller shades mounted outside the window frame, or add side channels so light cannot leak around the edges. In Central Ohio, cordless cellular shades run about $175 to $750 per window installed, and blackout fabric adds roughly 10 to 30 percent.

    Why blackout fabric alone isn't fully dark

    Blackout fabric stops light passing through the shade itself. Light still gets in around the shade: the small gaps at the sides, top and bottom. With an inside mount, those gaps are usually the biggest source of light in the morning.

    Three ways to close the gaps

    OptionHow it worksDarknessBest for
    Outside mountThe shade overlaps the window frame by a few inches on each sideVery darkBedrooms where you don't mind covering the trim
    Side channelsTracks on each side hold the shade edgeDarkestNurseries, shift workers, home theaters
    Blackout cellular, tight inside fitHoneycomb cells block light and sit close to the frameDark, with thin edge linesKeeping the window trim visible

    Blackout cellular vs. blackout roller shades

    Blackout cellular (honeycomb) shades also insulate, because their cells trap air at the glass. That makes them a strong pick for Ohio winters and for bedrooms over garages or on cold corners. Blackout roller shades have a cleaner, flatter look and come in more colors and textures, and they pair well with drapery for a layered look. See also our energy-efficient window treatments guide.

    Bedrooms where motorization helps

    Motorized blackout shades can close at bedtime and open at your alarm time on a schedule. They are especially useful for tall or hard-to-reach bedroom windows and for kids' rooms, since there are no cords. Rechargeable motors mean no new wiring in an existing home.     What it costs in Central Ohio

    • Cordless cellular shades: about $175 to $750 per window installed; blackout adds roughly 10 to 30 percent
    • Manual roller shades: about $175 to $650 per window installed
    • Rechargeable motorized roller shades: about $450 to $1,300 per window installed
    • Side channels and cassettes add cost; the exact price is confirmed at an in-home measure

    Frequently asked questions

    Do blackout shades make a room completely dark?

    The fabric blocks light, but light can leak around the edges. An outside mount or side channels gets a bedroom as close to fully dark as shades can.

    Are blackout cellular shades good for winter?

    Yes. The honeycomb cells insulate at the glass, which helps keep bedrooms warmer in Ohio winters.
