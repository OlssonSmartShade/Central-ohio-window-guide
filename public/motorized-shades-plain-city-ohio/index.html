Window Treatments for Plain City, Ohio Homes

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

Plain City has a mix of new construction, farmhouse-style homes, and older houses near the village center. New builds benefit most from planning ahead: prewire for motorized shades before drywall and cover bare windows with temporary privacy shades while custom treatments are made. Rural lots with open views and strong afternoon sun are a natural fit for solar and motorized shades.

What makes Plain City homes different

  • New construction: new neighborhoods around Plain City and nearby Jerome Township mean many families move in with every window bare at once.
  • Farmhouse and modern farmhouse styles: large black-framed windows, tall great rooms, and open sight lines, where woven wood shades, roller shades, and shutters suit the look.
  • Open lots and country views: fewer trees and neighbors mean strong direct sun, so solar shades that cut glare but keep the view are popular.
  • Older homes near the village center: smaller, sometimes out-of-square windows that need careful custom measuring.

Moving into a new build

  1. Before drywall: run wire to the top of any window you might motorize. It costs little during framing.
  2. After trim is installed: schedule the in-home measurement so sizes match the finished openings.
  3. Move-in day: temporary privacy shades cover bedrooms and bathrooms right away.
  4. A few weeks later: custom treatments go in, usually in one day.

Missed the prewire? Rechargeable motors need no wiring. See smart home window automation.

Farmhouse-style picks

  • Woven wood shades: natural texture that matches farmhouse decor, with optional privacy or blackout liners.
  • Roller and solar shades: a clean look on big black-framed windows, easy to motorize.
  • Plantation shutters: a classic, built-in feel. See shutter costs.

Frequently asked questions

How much do window treatments cost in Plain City, Ohio?

Faux wood blinds typically run $125 to $500 per window installed, cordless cellular shades $175 to $750, motorized rechargeable roller shades $450 to $1,300, and custom composite shutters $275 to $1,600.

Can I add motorized shades without rewiring?

Yes. Rechargeable battery motors give full app, voice, and schedule control with no new wiring. A charge typically lasts months between top-ups.

When should I prewire for motorized shades in a new Plain City home?

Before insulation and drywall go in. Have wire run to the top of each window you may want to motorize.

What window treatments suit a modern farmhouse?

Woven wood shades, roller or solar shades on large black-framed windows, and plantation shutters all fit the style.
