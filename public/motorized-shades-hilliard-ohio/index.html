Window Treatments for New and Existing Homes in Hilliard, Ohio

A local guide to window treatments for new and existing Hilliard, Ohio homes: covering bare windows in a new build, when to prewire motorized shades, budget plans by room, and 2026 costs.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated October 20263 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    If you're building in Hilliard, prewire for motorized shades before drywall and cover bare windows with temporary privacy shades while custom treatments are made. For an existing home, mix treatments by room to stay on budget: faux wood blinds in kitchens and baths, cordless cellular shades in bedrooms, and shutters or motorized shades in the main living space.

    What makes Hilliard homes different

    • New construction on the west and northwest sides: Hilliard keeps growing, and many owners move in with bare windows and a long list of rooms to finish at once.
    • 1970s to 2000s neighborhoods: established subdivisions where original builder blinds and patio-door verticals are ready for an upgrade.
    • Old Hilliard: the historic downtown near the Heritage Rail Trail has older homes with smaller, sometimes out-of-square windows that need careful custom measuring.

    Moving into a new build

    Custom treatments take a few weeks to make, so many installers offer free temporary privacy shades to cover bare windows in the meantime. A good order of operations:

    1. Before drywall: decide which windows might ever be motorized and have wire run to the top of each. It costs little now and saves opening walls later.
    2. After trim is installed: schedule the in-home measurement. Measuring earlier risks openings that don't match the finished frame.
    3. Move-in: temporary privacy shades go up so bedrooms and bathrooms are covered on day one.
    4. A few weeks later: custom treatments are installed, usually in a single day.

    Missed the prewire window? Rechargeable battery motors add full smart control with no wiring. See our smart home window automation guide.

    Budget-smart choices for the whole house

    Outfitting a whole house at once adds up fast. Mixing treatments by room keeps it manageable:

    • Kitchens and baths: faux wood blinds, which handle humidity and clean easily.
    • Bedrooms and nurseries: cordless blackout cellular shades. See child and pet safe options.
    • Main living area: shutters or motorized shades, where they get seen and used most.
    • Patio doors: vertical cellular shades or a motorized roller shade.
    • Spare rooms: start with temporary shades and finish later as budget allows.

    Frequently asked questions

    When should I prewire for motorized shades?

    Before insulation and drywall. If you miss it, rechargeable motors work with no wiring.

    When should windows be measured in a new build?

    After windows and trim are installed, so measurements match the finished openings.

    What do I do about bare windows on move-in day?

    Temporary privacy shades cover them until custom treatments arrive.

    Do I have to do the whole house at once?

    No. Many owners start with bedrooms and the main living area and finish the rest later.
