Motorized Shades and Custom Window Treatments in Westerville, Ohio

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

For most Westerville families, the biggest upgrade is swapping original corded blinds for cordless cellular shades, then adding motorized shades on the hardest-to-reach or most-used windows. Cordless cellular shades run about $175 to $750 per window installed, and motorized shades about $450 to $1,300.

What makes Westerville homes different

  • Uptown Westerville and the streets around Otterbein University: older homes, some over a century old, with tall narrow windows, original trim, and frames that may not be perfectly square. Custom shutters and inside-mounted shades preserve the character.
  • 1970s to 1990s subdivisions: the bulk of Westerville's housing. Many still have original corded mini blinds, vertical blinds on patio doors, and single or early double-pane glass. This is where most upgrades happen.
  • Newer homes on the north side and near Polaris: open floor plans, larger rear windows, and family rooms with tall glass that suit motorized shades.
  • Homes near Hoover Reservoir and Alum Creek: water and wooded views worth keeping, plus strong reflected glare. Solar shades cut glare while leaving the view open.

Family-safe and smart

Cordless and motorized treatments remove dangling cords entirely, the safest choice for homes with young kids and pets. Motorized shades can run on a schedule or by voice through Alexa, Google Home, or Apple Home, so nobody needs to reach behind furniture or climb on a couch to open a window. See child and pet safe cordless window treatments.

Upgrading original builder blinds

If your home still has its original blinds, a room-by-room plan keeps the cost manageable:

  1. Kids' rooms and nurseries first: cordless blackout cellular shades for safety and better sleep.
  2. Patio doors next: replace old vertical blinds with vertical cellular shades or a motorized roller shade.
  3. Main living areas: shutters or motorized shades where the family spends the most time.
  4. Kitchens and baths: faux wood blinds or moisture-resistant shutters that handle humidity.

Popular choices for Westerville homes

Cordless cellular shades insulate well through Ohio winters, faux wood blinds stand up to kitchens and baths, and composite shutters hold up to busy households with kids and dogs. Compare them in shades vs blinds vs shutters, and see how to automate in our smart home window automation guide.

Frequently asked questions

What are the safest window treatments for kids?

Cordless and motorized options, which have no reachable cords or loops.

Is it worth replacing original builder blinds?

Usually yes. Cordless cellular shades improve safety, insulation, and looks, and it's one of the most visible low-cost upgrades in an older subdivision home.

What should go on a sliding patio door?

Vertical cellular shades, sliding panels, or a motorized roller shade all replace old vertical blinds cleanly.

Can I motorize just a few windows?

Yes. Many families start with one or two hard-to-reach windows and add more later; rechargeable motors make that easy.
