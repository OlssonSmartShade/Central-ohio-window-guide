Window Treatments in Columbus, Ohio: Custom Blinds, Shades and Shutters

A local guide to window treatments and window coverings in Columbus, Ohio: brick homes in German Village and Clintonville, condos in the Short North and downtown, custom blinds, motorized shades, and 2026 installed costs.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated October 20264 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    Columbus homes range from century-old brick houses to downtown condos with floor-to-ceiling glass, so the right window treatment depends on the building. Older homes usually need careful custom measuring for out-of-square windows, while condos and newer builds are where motorized and solar shades shine. Expect roughly $125 to $500 per window for custom blinds and $450 to $1,300 for rechargeable motorized shades, installed. Olsson Smart Shades offers free in-home consultations across Columbus.

    What makes Columbus homes different

    • Historic brick homes: neighborhoods like German Village, Victorian Village, and Italian Village have older windows that are often out of square, deep set, or original. Custom inside-mount shades and shutters fit these best, and shallow frames may need outside mounts.
    • Clintonville and the north side: 1920s to 1950s homes with lots of double-hung windows, where cordless cellular shades and shutters are popular for insulation and a classic look.
    • Short North and downtown condos: tall glass walls and city views, where solar shades cut glare without blocking the skyline and motorized shades reach windows you cannot reach by hand.
    • Newer suburbs inside the city: open floor plans, patio doors, and two-story great rooms, often a good fit for motorization.

    Custom blinds and window coverings in Columbus

    Custom blinds are built to each window's exact size, so they hang straight and close fully, which store-bought blinds rarely do in older Columbus homes. Faux wood blinds are the workhorse for kitchens, baths, and rentals because they resist humidity and clean easily. Real wood blinds suit German Village and Clintonville living rooms. Aluminum mini blinds give condos a slim modern look. Beyond blinds, popular Columbus window coverings include cellular shades for insulation, roller and solar shades for condos, Roman and woven wood shades for warmth, and plantation shutters for a lasting upgrade.

    Condos and apartments

    Many Columbus condo buildings have rules about how window treatments look from outside, often requiring a white or neutral backing. Check your association's rules before ordering. Rechargeable motorized shades, also called automated blinds, are a good fit for condos because they need no new wiring. See the smart home window automation guide.

    Older windows need careful measuring

    In historic homes, measure each window at the top, middle, and bottom, since they often differ. Ordering by a single measurement is the most common reason custom treatments don't fit. Shutters can be built for out-of-square openings, and outside mounts can cover frames that are too shallow.

    Choosing a window treatment company in Columbus

    Columbus has national franchises, big-box installers, and local dealers. A few things separate a good window treatment company from the rest: it measures every window in person, brings real samples to your home so you see them in your own light, installs with its own trained team instead of subcontractors, and gives a clear written quote.
