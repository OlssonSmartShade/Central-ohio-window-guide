Window Treatments and Motorized Shades in Columbus, Ohio

A local guide to window treatments in Columbus, Ohio: brick homes in German Village and Clintonville, condos in the Short North and downtown, motorized shades, and 2026 installed costs.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated October 20263 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    Columbus homes range from century-old brick houses to downtown condos with floor-to-ceiling glass, so the right treatment depends on the building. Older homes usually need careful custom measuring for out-of-square windows, while condos and newer builds are where motorized and solar shades shine. Expect roughly $125 to $500 per window for blinds and $450 to $1,300 for rechargeable motorized shades, installed.

    What makes Columbus homes different

    • Historic brick homes: neighborhoods like German Village, Victorian Village, and Italian Village have older windows that are often out of square, deep set, or original. Custom inside-mount shades and shutters fit these best, and shallow frames may need outside mounts.
    • Clintonville and the north side: 1920s to 1950s homes with lots of double-hung windows, where cordless cellular shades and shutters are popular for insulation and a classic look.
    • Short North and downtown condos: tall glass walls and city views, where solar shades cut glare without blocking the skyline and motorized shades reach windows you cannot reach by hand.
    • Newer suburbs inside the city: open floor plans, patio doors, and two-story great rooms, often a good fit for motorization.

    Condos and apartments

    Many Columbus condo buildings have rules about how window treatments look from outside, often requiring a white or neutral backing. Check your association's rules before ordering. Rechargeable motorized shades are a good fit for condos because they need no new wiring. See the smart home window automation guide.

    Older windows need careful measuring

    In historic homes, measure each window at the top, middle, and bottom, since they often differ. Ordering by a single measurement is the most common reason custom treatments don't fit. Shutters can be built for out-of-square openings, and outside mounts can cover frames that are too shallow.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much do window treatments cost in Columbus, Ohio?

    Faux wood blinds typically run $125 to $500 per window installed, cordless cellular shades $175 to $750, motorized rechargeable roller shades $450 to $1,300, and custom composite shutters $275 to $1,600.

    Can I add motorized shades without rewiring?

    Yes. Rechargeable battery motors give full app, voice, and schedule control with no new wiring. A charge typically lasts months between top-ups.

    What window treatments work best in historic Columbus homes?

    Custom inside-mount shades and plantation shutters fit older, out-of-square windows best. Each window should be measured at the top, middle, and bottom before ordering.

    Are there rules for window treatments in Columbus condos?

    Many condo associations require a white or neutral backing visible from outside. Check your association's rules before ordering.
