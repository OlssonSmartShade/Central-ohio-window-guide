Motorized Shades and Custom Window Treatments in Upper Arlington, Ohio

Updated October 2026

Upper Arlington is known for classic and historic-style homes, where custom-fit treatments matter on older, sometimes out-of-square windows. This guide covers what works best, what it costs installed, and how to get it done right.

Adding smart shades to an older home

You don't need to open plaster walls to automate an older home. Rechargeable battery motors add full smart control with no wiring, working with Apple Home, Alexa, Google Home, and SmartThings through the Matter-over-Thread standard. More in our smart home window automation guide.

Popular choices for Upper Arlington homes

Custom plantation shutters suit classic architecture and can be built to fit arched or out-of-square openings. Roman and cellular shades add warmth and insulation against drafty older glass. Compare options in shades vs blinds vs shutters.

What it costs in Upper Arlington

Motorized rechargeable roller shades typically run $450 to $1,300 per window installed, cordless cellular shades $175 to $750, composite shutters $275 to $1,600, and hardwood shutters $325 to $1,950. Specialty shapes run 15 to 40 percent more. Full breakdown: window treatment costs in Central Ohio.
