Motorized Shades and Custom Window Treatments in Upper Arlington, Ohio

A local guide to motorized shades, plantation shutters, and cellular shades for Upper Arlington, Ohio homes, from 1920s Tudors and Colonials to mid-century ranches and new rebuilds: what works and what it costs.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated October 20263 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    In Upper Arlington's older homes, the best results usually come from custom plantation shutters or insulating cellular shades built to each window's exact size, with rechargeable motorized shades added where you want smart control without touching plaster walls. Expect roughly $275 to $1,950 per window for shutters and $450 to $1,300 for motorized shades installed.

    What makes Upper Arlington homes different

    Upper Arlington is one of Columbus's oldest planned suburbs, and its housing falls into three broad groups:

    • 1920s to 1940s Tudors, Colonials, and cottages: found in the original neighborhoods near Northam Park and Tremont Road. Expect deep window sills, divided-light windows, arches, plaster walls, and frames that are often slightly out of square after decades of settling.
    • Mid-century ranches and split-levels: common across the northern and western parts of the city. Long horizontal windows, sliders, and picture windows that often face the street.
    • New rebuilds: Upper Arlington sees a steady stream of teardowns and new construction on established lots. These homes have large modern windows and are the easiest place to plan hardwired motorized shades from the start.

    Adding smart shades to an older home

    You don't need to open plaster walls or disturb original trim to automate a 1930s house. Rechargeable battery motors add full smart control with no wiring and work with Apple Home, Alexa, Google Home, and SmartThings through the Matter-over-Thread standard. Set them to close on summer afternoons and open at sunrise, and the house stays cooler without anyone touching a cord. More in our smart home window automation guide.

    Measuring older windows the right way

    Out-of-square windows are the most common problem in older Upper Arlington homes. A good installer measures width at the top, middle, and bottom and height at both sides, then builds to the smallest dimension for inside mounts. Shutters can be scribed to follow an uneven frame. If a shallow or uneven sill makes an inside mount look wrong, an outside mount on the trim is often the cleaner choice.

    Best picks by home type

    • Tudors and Colonials: hardwood or composite plantation shutters for a classic look, or Roman shades in fabric for living and dining rooms.
    • Drafty original glass: cellular shades, which trap air in honeycomb pockets and noticeably cut winter drafts.
    • Ranch picture windows and sliders: motorized roller or cellular shades, or vertical cellular shades on sliding doors.
    • New rebuilds: hardwired motorized shades planned before drywall, with pockets or valances to hide the hardware.
    • Arches and specialty shapes: custom-built shutters or fixed shapes. See sunrooms and hard-to-cover windows.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I add smart shades without opening plaster walls?

    Yes. Rechargeable motors need no wiring and recharge with a USB cable every few months.

    What works best for out-of-square windows?

    Custom plantation shutters, which can be scribed to fit uneven frames. Custom shades also work if each window is measured at several points.

    Are shutters worth it in an older home?

    They suit classic architecture, add insulation against older glass, and are usually seen as a lasting feature of the home.

    Should I replace old windows first?

    If you plan to replace windows within a year or two, do that first, since new frames change the measurements. Otherwise, cellular shades or shutters can make older windows far more comfortable now.
