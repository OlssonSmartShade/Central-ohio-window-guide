Exterior Motorized Screens for Ohio Patios and Porches

A motorized screen turns a hot, buggy patio into a room you can use. Here is how they work, what to look for, and how they handle Ohio weather.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated November 20263 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    Motorized exterior screens drop down over a patio, porch, or large window from a housing above. The best ones, like the Draper FlexShade ZIP, hold the fabric edge inside side channels so there are no gaps for sun, rain, or insects, and they stop automatically if they touch an obstacle. They are controlled by remote, app, or smart home system.

    Side channels vs. side cables

    Some exterior shades are guided by thin cables, which leave a gap along each side. Draper notes that sun, rain, insects, small birds and small animals can get through those gaps. Side-channel (ZIP) screens hold the fabric edge inside a track, so the screen seals along both sides.

    How they handle Ohio wind and weather

    Draper tested the FlexShade ZIP at the Wall of Wind lab and says it can safely withstand winds up to 100 mph, with some test units surviving much higher speeds. Draper still recommends raising exterior shades in strong winds because of flying debris, and most homeowners raise them before a storm.

    Safety and controls

    The FlexShade ZIP uses an obstruction-detection motor: when the bottom bar touches something such as debris, a pet or a child, the motor stops. It runs on Somfy motors, so it can be controlled by a handheld remote, an app or a smart home system, and grouped with interior motorized shades. See the smart home window automation guide.

    Where they work best in Central Ohio

    • Covered patios and porches that get hot afternoon sun
    • Pergolas and outdoor living rooms where you want shade and bug protection
    • Large west-facing windows, where stopping heat outside the glass keeps rooms cooler

    Source: Draper, Five reasons to choose the DraperHome FlexShade ZIP.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do exterior screens keep bugs out?

    Side-channel ZIP screens close along both edges, which keeps out insects that get past cable-guided shades.

    Can exterior shades stay down in the wind?

    Draper's testing found the FlexShade ZIP withstands winds up to 100 mph, but Draper advises raising shades in strong winds because of flying debris.
