Child and Pet Safe Cordless Window Treatments: A Central Ohio Guide

Updated October 2026

Window cords are a well-documented strangulation hazard for young children, and they can tangle pets too. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recommends cordless window coverings in homes with young children. The good news is that going cordless no longer means giving up style or convenience.

Why cordless matters

Older blinds and shades use pull cords and continuous loops that hang within reach. Cord cleats and tie-downs help, but they rely on someone remembering to use them every time. Cordless treatments remove the hazard entirely.

The safest options

Cordless lift shades and blinds raise and lower by hand from the bottom rail, with nothing hanging down. They're available in cellular, roller, Roman, and faux wood styles.

Motorized shades are the cleanest option of all: no cords, controlled by remote, phone, voice, or schedule. Rechargeable battery motors mean no wiring is needed in an existing home. Learn more in our smart window automation guide.

Plantation shutters have no cords at all and hold up well to curious hands and paws.

Pet-friendly tips

Dogs that watch the window often bend or chew blind slats. Shutters, roller shades, and top-down/bottom-up cellular shades hold up better, and top-down styles let you keep the bottom of the window open for a pet's view while keeping privacy above.

Room-by-room picks

Nurseries and kids' rooms: cordless or motorized blackout cellular shades. Living areas: motorized roller shades or shutters. Rooms where pets keep watch: shutters or top-down/bottom-up shades. For pricing, see window treatment costs in Central Ohio.
