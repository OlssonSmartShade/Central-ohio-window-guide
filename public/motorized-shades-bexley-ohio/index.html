Window Treatments for Historic and Older Homes in Bexley, Ohio

A local guide to window treatments in Bexley, Ohio: Tudor and Colonial Revival homes along East Broad and near Capital University, leaded glass, plantation shutters, motorized shades, and 2026 costs.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated October 20263 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    Bexley is known for older, character-rich homes, so the key is a precise custom fit that respects original windows and trim. Plantation shutters and custom cellular or Roman shades are common picks, and rechargeable motorized shades add smart control without opening plaster walls. Expect about $275 to $1,600 per window for composite shutters and $450 to $1,300 for rechargeable motorized shades, installed.

    What makes Bexley homes different

    • East Broad Street and the north side: large Tudor Revival, Colonial Revival, and Georgian homes, including the stretch near the Ohio Governor's Residence. Expect leaded casements, tall living-room windows, and formal rooms where shutters and Roman shades suit the architecture.
    • Around Capital University and Main Street: early and mid-1900s homes with original wood double-hungs, deep sills, and detailed trim worth showing off.
    • Near Jeffrey Park and Alum Creek: wooded lots and creek views, where light-filtering or solar shades cut glare without hiding the trees.
    • Out-of-square openings: settling over decades means windows often vary by a quarter inch or more top to bottom.
    • Plaster walls: running new wiring is harder than in newer homes, which makes battery-powered motors attractive.

    Getting the fit right

    Measure each window in three places and order to the smallest width for inside mounts. Deep original frames are ideal for inside-mount shades and shutters that sit flush with the trim. If a frame is too shallow or uneven, an outside mount or a shutter frame built to the opening can cover the difference cleanly.

    Leaded, stained, and arched glass

    Bexley's feature windows are part of what makes the homes special. Many owners leave them uncovered, add a nearly invisible film to cut UV and protect floors and furniture, or mount a shade that clears the glass so it never rubs the lead came. See home window film and hard-to-cover windows.

    Popular choices in older homes

    • Plantation shutters: a classic look that suits traditional architecture. See shutter costs.
    • Cordless cellular shades: extra insulation for older, draftier windows in Ohio winters.
    • Roman shades: tailored fabric that fits formal living and dining rooms.
    • Rechargeable motorized shades: also called automated blinds, they add smart control with no rewiring through plaster. See smart home window automation.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much do window treatments cost in Bexley, Ohio?

    Faux wood blinds typically run $125 to $500 per window installed, cordless cellular shades $175 to $750, motorized rechargeable roller shades $450 to $1,300, and custom composite shutters $275 to $1,600.

    Can I add motorized shades without rewiring?

    Yes. Rechargeable battery motors give full app, voice, and schedule control with no new wiring. A charge typically lasts months between top-ups.

    What window treatments work best in older Bexley homes?

    Custom inside-mount plantation shutters, cellular shades, and Roman shades fit older homes well, as long as each window is measured in several places before ordering.

    Should I cover leaded or stained glass windows?

    Often not. Many owners leave feature windows uncovered or use a discreet film or a shade mounted to clear the glass.
