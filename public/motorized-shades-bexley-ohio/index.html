Window Treatments for Historic and Older Homes in Bexley, Ohio

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

Bexley is known for older, character-rich homes, so the key is a precise custom fit that respects original windows and trim. Plantation shutters and custom cellular or Roman shades are common picks, and rechargeable motorized shades add smart control without opening plaster walls.

What makes Bexley homes different

  • Older and historic homes: many houses date to the early and mid 1900s, with original wood windows, deep sills, and detailed trim worth showing off.
  • Out-of-square openings: settling over decades means windows often vary by a quarter inch or more top to bottom.
  • Leaded, stained, and arched glass: feature windows that are often best left uncovered or treated with a discreet film or a shade mounted to clear the glass.
  • Plaster walls: running new wiring is harder than in newer homes, which makes battery-powered motors attractive.

Getting the fit right

Measure each window in three places and order to the smallest width for inside mounts. Deep original frames are ideal for inside-mount shades and shutters that sit flush with the trim. If a frame is too shallow or uneven, an outside mount or a shutter frame built to the opening can cover the difference cleanly.

Popular choices in older homes

  • Plantation shutters: a classic look that suits traditional architecture. See shutter costs.
  • Cordless cellular shades: extra insulation for older, draftier windows in Ohio winters.
  • Roman shades: tailored fabric that fits formal rooms.
  • Rechargeable motorized shades: smart control with no rewiring through plaster. See smart home window automation.

Frequently asked questions

How much do window treatments cost in Bexley, Ohio?

Faux wood blinds typically run $125 to $500 per window installed, cordless cellular shades $175 to $750, motorized rechargeable roller shades $450 to $1,300, and custom composite shutters $275 to $1,600.

Can I add motorized shades without rewiring?

Yes. Rechargeable battery motors give full app, voice, and schedule control with no new wiring. A charge typically lasts months between top-ups.

What window treatments work best in older Bexley homes?

Custom inside-mount plantation shutters, cellular shades, and Roman shades fit older homes well, as long as each window is measured in several places before ordering.

Should I cover leaded or stained glass windows?

Often not. Many owners leave feature windows uncovered or use a discreet film or a shade mounted to clear the glass.
