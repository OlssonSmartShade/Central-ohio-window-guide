Window Treatments for Lewis Center, Ohio Homes

A local guide to window treatments in Lewis Center, Ohio: blackout and light-filtering roller shades, layering with drapery, new construction, motorized shades, and 2026 installed costs.

Central Ohio Window GuideUpdated October 20263 min read

In this guide

    Quick answer

    Lewis Center is mostly newer family neighborhoods, so the common needs are privacy from close neighbors, dark bedrooms, and covering many windows at once on move-in. Roller shades layered under drapery are a popular bedroom setup, and rechargeable motorized shades add smart control with no wiring.

    What makes Lewis Center homes different

    • Newer construction: many homes were built in the last two decades, with standard-size double-hung windows that are easy to fit with inside-mount shades.
    • Close neighbors: street-facing bedrooms and side windows need privacy without living in the dark.
    • Families with kids: cordless and motorized treatments are the safe choice. See child and pet safe options.

    The layered bedroom setup

    An inside-mount roller shade handles daily privacy and light control, and side drapery panels add softness and block light leaking at the edges. Blackout fabric suits nurseries and bedrooms for shift workers, while light-filtering fabric keeps daytime rooms bright.

    Moving into a new build

    1. Prewire any window you might motorize before drywall.
    2. Measure after windows and trim are installed.
    3. Cover bedrooms and baths with temporary privacy shades on move-in day.
    4. Finish the main living areas first, then the rest as budget allows.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much do window treatments cost in Lewis Center, Ohio?

    Faux wood blinds typically run $125 to $500 per window installed, cordless cellular shades $175 to $750, motorized rechargeable roller shades $450 to $1,300, and custom composite shutters $275 to $1,600.

    Can I add motorized shades without rewiring?

    Yes. Rechargeable battery motors give full app, voice, and schedule control with no new wiring. A charge typically lasts months between top-ups.

    What's the best window treatment for a dark bedroom?

    An inside-mount blackout roller or cellular shade, often layered with drapery panels that cover light gaps at the edges.

    Do I need to do every window at once?

    No. Many owners start with bedrooms and the main living area, using temporary shades elsewhere until later.
